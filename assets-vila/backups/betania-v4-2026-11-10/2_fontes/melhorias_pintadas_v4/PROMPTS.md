## Terra clara — terra_pintada_v4

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: seamless square albedo terrain texture for a low poly 3D ancient Mediterranean village game.
Primary request: pale dry limestone dust soil, compacted walking earth with a few tiny chalk chips, NOT stone paving. Hand painted digital gouache with visibly broad angular dry-brush strokes, restrained flat faceted patches, only 4 main tones. Palette base #CDB48C, light #E2CFA8, muted shade #A88E6A, occasional cool mauve undertone. Quiet medium-scale detail readable from an isometric camera. Even distribution, no focal point. Perfect orthographic top view, flat albedo, no baked lighting, no cast shadows, no bevels, no vignette. Fills entire 1024x1024 square. Seamless tileable on all four edges, edge-to-edge consistent colors. No reddish soil, no vegetation, no objects, no text, no watermark. Clearly handmade digital brush strokes, never photorealistic.

## Reboco — reboco_pintado_v4

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: seamless albedo texture for limestone plaster walls on low poly 3D ancient Judean village houses.
Primary request: hand-troweled pale warm ivory lime plaster with broad clearly visible DIGITAL GOUACHE BRUSH STROKES, flat angular planes and scraped painterly scumbles. Restrained 4-color palette chalk #E6D6B5, base #D9C7A3, muted ochre #BFA57E and sparse cool mauve-gray strokes. Large quiet irregular patches, subtle material variation; avoid strong cracks or grids, no bricks, no rocks, no holes. Clearly painted illustration, not photographic. Uniform orthographic flat albedo, no lighting or cast shadows, no highlights, no vignette, no perspective. Square image fills entire frame 1024x1024; seamless tileable across all four edges, no border. No text or watermark. Surface must read as ancient hand-painted plaster with obvious expressive broad digital brushwork, while leaving geometry to define faceting.
